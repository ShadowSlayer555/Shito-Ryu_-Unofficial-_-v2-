About & Contact

The Creation of This Portal

This website was created by a practiioner of karate to serve as a resource for Shito-Ryu Karate practitioners. Recognizing the need for an organized library of Katas, Techniques, Kumite drills, and terminology, this portal aims to help students learn alongside the teachings of their dojo.

By consolidating demonstration videos from respected instructors into a single platform, we hope to support your journey on the path of Karate-do.
